Changelog

What changed in each version of Logilator. The newest version is at the top.

v15.5 Latest
Nov 2026
  • NewSettings panel from the gear at the top left: accent colour and profit/loss colours (presets and your own colour pickers), including a colour-blind friendly blue and orange option.
  • NewInterface size (compact, default, large), and switches for the cursor glow, the bouncing dollar sign and animations.
  • NewBreakeven threshold and keyboard shortcuts can be changed in Settings.
  • ImprovedThe Trade History and Journal buttons are gone from the calculator page. Both open from the tabs at the top.
v15.4
Nov 2026
  • ImprovedBreakeven now means a trade that closed within 0.1% of your account balance. Profits and losses outside that range are never marked breakeven.
  • NewAny trade can be marked or unmarked as breakeven by hand with a small button, in case it was classed wrongly.
v15.3
Nov 2026
  • ImprovedJournal opens from its own tab. The calculator page is simpler.
  • FixedOn phones the journal statistics no longer push the trade list off the screen, and the trade details open as a panel.
v15.2
Nov 2026
  • NewColour in the journal: green for profit, red for loss, grey for breakeven.
  • NewEvery trade is kept permanently. Nothing is dropped after 7 days, and recent history is imported from Bybit the first time.
  • ImprovedDark scrollbars that match the theme (they were bright white before).
  • FixedProfit and loss amounts briefly lost their colours after the stats redesign.
v15.1
Nov 2026
  • NewJournal statistics: win rate, breakeven rate and loss rate, average leverage, average risk per trade, average reward-to-risk, average gain, and total fees.
  • ImprovedA cleaner journal layout with one summary panel and a row of stat cards.
  • FixedPressing Tab in the Logic AI box now moves on to the Journal instead of back to the calculator. Tab cycles Calculator, Logic AI, Journal.
v15.0
Nov 2026
  • NewTrade journal. Every trade is recorded when it closes, matched with the plan you reviewed: planned entry, exit, size and risk next to what actually happened.
  • NewNotes, tags and a star rating for each trade, search and filters, and CSV export.
  • NewDelete single or multiple trades, or clear the whole trade history, with a confirmation step.
v14.0
  • NewChart screenshot reading in Logic AI. Paste, drop or attach a screenshot of your long/short tool and it reads the entry, stop loss and take profit from the price axis, then shows a card to check. Nothing changes until you press Apply, and a screenshot never starts an order.
v13.0
  • NewKeyboard shortcuts: “/” jumps to the chat box and Tab switches views.
v12.0
  • NewLogic AI became a full chat page with its own tab: saved chats, a model picker that browses the OpenRouter catalogue (free and paid, with context size and prices), live streaming answers, and a stop button.
  • NewEvery answer shows which model replied, how long it took and the tokens used. “Thought for” opens the reasoning where the model provides it.
  • NewAsk it to place a long or short and it fills the calculator and opens the normal order review. Nothing is sent until you confirm.
  • ImprovedThe floating chat button was replaced by the top tabs, Calculator and Logic AI.
v11.0
  • NewAssistant chat powered by your own OpenRouter key. It answers questions about your numbers and changes the calculator fields on command, each with an Undo link. It never sees your Bybit keys.
Before 11.0
  • NewThe calculator: position size, notional, minimum leverage, reward-to-risk, liquidation check, and net profit and loss after fees.
  • NewOrder placement to Bybit from your own PC, with a review window before every order. Cancel and edit orders, live positions with partial closes, and a basic trade history.
  • NewSafety checks on every order: isolated margin, one-way mode, stop loss versus liquidation, maximum risk per trade and an optional daily loss limit.
