You can lose everything

Trading crypto futures carries a high level of risk and is not suitable for everyone. Leverage magnifies both gains and losses, and you can lose your entire deposit, in some cases quickly. Prices can move past your stop loss, orders can fill at worse prices than planned (slippage), and positions can be liquidated. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.

Speed does not remove risk

Logilator is built to help you place an order quickly, but fast is not the same as safe or profitable. Placing a limit order faster does not guarantee that it fills, that it fills at your price, or that the trade wins. Rushing is a common cause of oversized positions, wrong levels and wrong directions. Take the time to read the review window before you confirm, and use the safety limits in your settings.

What Logilator is

Logilator is a calculator and order-entry tool. It helps you size a position from the risk you choose, but it cannot tell you whether a trade is a good idea, and it does not make you profitable. Its figures, including fees, liquidation estimates and reward-to-risk, are estimates based on the numbers you enter and on Bybit's published rules, and they can be wrong. Bybit's own figures always take priority. Nothing on this website or in the software is financial advice.

AI and automation

Logic AI uses third-party language models that can misread a chart or make mistakes. It shows what it found and waits for your confirmation, but the responsibility for every level and every order is yours. Check each number against your own chart before you confirm.

Before you trade live

  • Practice on Bybit Demo Trading or the testnet first.
  • Use an API key with trading permission only, never withdrawals, restricted to your IP address if possible.
  • Set a maximum risk per trade and a daily loss limit in your settings.
  • Start with small amounts and risk only what you can afford to lose.
  • If you're unsure, speak to a qualified, independent financial adviser.
